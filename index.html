Pagan Survivor

A one-thumb Survivors-like roguelite set in the pagan Baltic lands. Play a chosen champion of the old gods and hold out against hordes from Lithuanian folklore: vilkatai, laumės, restless vėlės and the servants of Velnias. Build your powers each run, then earn lasting blessings from Perkūnas, Laima and Žemyna between runs.
