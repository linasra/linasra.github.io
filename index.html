Pagan Survivor

A roguelite survival game set in the pagan Baltic lands. Play a chosen champion of the old gods and hold out against hordes from Lithuanian folklore: vilkatai, laumės, restless vėlės and the servants of Velnias. Craft a new build every run, then earn lasting blessings from Perkūnas, Laima and Žemyna.

  • Lithuanian mythology
  • Low-poly 3D
  • Build crafting
  • Short runs
